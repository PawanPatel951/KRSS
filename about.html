Who We Are

A people-first approach to protection.

Knight Riders Safety Solutions is a growing protection and safety organisation built on long-term relationships, maintained through an inclusive, personal approach to every client.

Our motto is to safeguard life and property from disasters, while raising the standard of security and safeguarding services.

OUR PROMISE

Safeguarding lives, property and trust.

Our motto is to safeguard life and property from any kind of disaster that may occur, while striving to set higher benchmarks in securing and safeguarding.

Our vision is to change how people perceive the security industry through lasting commitment and dependable service. At the centre of this philosophy are our people: “A Happy Employee is an Efficient Employee.”
