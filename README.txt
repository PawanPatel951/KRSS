KRSS PREMIUM MULTI-PAGE WEBSITE
================================
Pages: index.html, about.html, security.html, facility.html, fire-safety.html, gallery.html, contact.html

Open index.html in a browser or drag the full extracted folder to Netlify Drop for hosting.
All local photos in assets/ come from the client-provided Runwal_PPT.zip. Company profile PDF included.
Google Fonts are loaded from a CDN; the local pages otherwise work without a build process.

BEFORE GOING LIVE:
1. Confirm KRSS current company name, PSARA/MSME registration, phone, address and service claims.
2. Ask permission to publish client-site/training photos, personnel and the company profile PDF.
3. Link the form to a real backend (Netlify Forms, Formspree, or your API). Demo currently opens mailto.
4. Verify WhatsApp number is enabled before directing traffic.
5. Add approved privacy policy, privacy consent, site analytics and cookie handling if needed.
6. Optional: add approved client logos and professional photos once supplied.

The website intentionally does not invent customer ratings, partnerships or company statistics.
